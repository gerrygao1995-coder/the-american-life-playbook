# Twelve situations, twelve action checklists

These lists organize the guide. They are not additional numbered plays, and they do not create eligibility or extend a deadline. Use the linked entries for conditions and sources. In immediate danger call 911; for suicide or mental-health crisis support call or text 988.

## 1. I lost my job

- [ ] Save pay stubs, benefits information, separation dates, and your own lawful records. Do not take confidential employer files.
- [ ] Apply through the relevant state unemployment system and read the reporting instructions. [Unemployment](guide/14-job-loss-and-assistance.md#141-apply-for-unemployment-through-the-correct-state).
- [ ] Compare replacement coverage and enrollment windows. [Health coverage](guide/14-job-loss-and-assistance.md#142-compare-health-coverage-before-the-enrollment-window-closes).
- [ ] Prioritize essential bills; check food, health, and energy assistance. [Essentials](guide/08-debt-scams-setbacks.md#081-protect-essentials-when-there-is-not-enough-money) · [Assistance](guide/14-job-loss-and-assistance.md#143-check-food-health-and-energy-help-together).
- [ ] Review any severance agreement before signing; calendar the actual deadline. [Severance](guide/14-job-loss-and-assistance.md#144-save-separation-records-before-reviewing-a-severance-agreement).
- [ ] Update one resume and reconnect with a useful contact. [Job-search kit](guide/33-career-growth-and-negotiation.md#335-keep-a-job-search-kit-ready-while-you-are-employed).

## 2. I received a large medical bill

- [ ] Compare the bill with the insurer's explanation of benefits; they are different documents.
- [ ] Request an itemized review and check patient details, services, adjustments, and payments.
- [ ] Ask about financial assistance and the application deadline before using expensive credit. [Bill review](guide/04-health-insurance.md#044-request-bill-review-and-financial-assistance-before-borrowing).
- [ ] If coverage was denied, identify the reason and appeal deadline. [Insurance appeal](guide/04-health-insurance.md#045-appeal-a-denial-using-the-reason-and-deadline).
- [ ] Keep names, dates, reference numbers, copies, and written confirmation of any payment arrangement.
- [ ] Use [the bill-review script](TEMPLATES.md#1-medical-bill-review). A request does not automatically stop collection or preserve an appeal deadline.

## 3. I am comparing job offers

- [ ] Write down base pay, variable pay conditions, schedule, location, and commute.
- [ ] Request benefit costs, coverage start dates, leave terms, retirement match, and vesting. [Total compensation](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#135-compare-offers-using-a-full-compensation-worksheet).
- [ ] Check whether the job requires a license or a realistically obtainable credential.
- [ ] Research comparable work in the local market. [Pay research](guide/33-career-growth-and-negotiation.md#331-research-the-pay-range-for-the-work-you-actually-do).
- [ ] Ask for a specific adjustment and get the final terms in writing. [Negotiation](guide/33-career-growth-and-negotiation.md#332-make-a-specific-compensation-request-before-accepting).
- [ ] Compare the household budget after taxes and new expenses, especially if moving. [Move comparison](guide/30-major-decisions-moving.md#302-compare-a-move-using-take-home-pay-and-actual-expenses).

## 4. I am moving or choosing a home

- [ ] Compare total monthly cost and upfront cash, including transport, utilities, and deposits. [Housing budget](guide/11-renting-buying-home.md#111-set-a-housing-budget-that-survives-the-move).
- [ ] Read the lease or purchase terms; document condition and responsibilities. [Rental records](guide/11-renting-buying-home.md#112-read-the-lease-and-document-the-condition).
- [ ] Price the relevant insurance before committing. [Disaster coverage](guide/11-renting-buying-home.md#115-price-disaster-coverage-before-committing-to-a-home).
- [ ] Verify an interstate mover and get clear written terms. [Moving services](guide/30-major-decisions-moving.md#303-verify-an-interstate-mover-before-paying-a-deposit).
- [ ] Plan utility changes, important mail, medication access, and a gap between homes.
- [ ] Review registrations, insurance, and benefits affected by the new location. [Identity and civic paperwork](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md).

## 5. We are having a baby

- [ ] Arrange prenatal care and review medicines with the clinician. [Prenatal care](guide/06-pregnancy-infancy.md#061-arrange-prenatal-care-and-review-medicines-early).
- [ ] Confirm expected delivery coverage and the process for newborn enrollment. [Coverage](guide/06-pregnancy-infancy.md#062-plan-coverage-for-delivery-and-enroll-the-newborn-promptly).
- [ ] Ask separately about protected leave, paid leave, and disability benefits. [Leave](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#132-ask-about-protected-leave-before-using-up-your-options).
- [ ] Prepare an appropriate car seat and a safe, separate sleep space. [Car seat](guide/06-pregnancy-infancy.md#064-install-the-car-seat-and-check-its-fit-before-the-first-ride) · [Infant sleep](guide/06-pregnancy-infancy.md#063-set-up-an-empty-separate-sleep-space).
- [ ] Assign practical postpartum help and learn the warning signs. [Postpartum plan](guide/06-pregnancy-infancy.md#065-make-postpartum-support-and-warning-signs-part-of-the-plan).
- [ ] Compare child care and possible assistance before the return-to-work date. [Child care](guide/20-children-and-education.md#201-compare-child-care-with-assistance-included).

## 6. I received a chronic-condition diagnosis

- [ ] Ask what the diagnosis means, what needs action now, and what the next visit should resolve.
- [ ] Make one medication list including over-the-counter products and supplements. [Medication safety](guide/05-chronic-illness.md#051-keep-one-medication-list-and-check-changes).
- [ ] Ask for a short written care plan with symptoms that need urgent attention. [Care plan](guide/05-chronic-illness.md#053-turn-a-chronic-condition-plan-into-a-short-checklist).
- [ ] Compare appropriate medicine options and actual out-of-pocket cost. [Medicine prices](guide/05-chronic-illness.md#052-ask-about-an-appropriate-generic-and-the-actual-price).
- [ ] Check coverage and work accommodations if needed. [Planned care](guide/04-health-insurance.md#043-confirm-the-network-and-expected-charges-before-planned-care) · [Accommodations](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#134-request-accommodations-and-track-discrimination-deadlines).
- [ ] Avoid making a major treatment change solely because an online testimonial sounds convincing. [Evaluate health claims](guide/28-health-claims.md).

## 7. I think I was scammed

- [ ] Contact the payment provider quickly using a verified number; explain the payment method and what happened. Recovery is not guaranteed. [Scam response](guide/08-debt-scams-setbacks.md#085-interrupt-a-scam-and-act-quickly-after-a-loss).
- [ ] Secure affected accounts, starting with email and finances, through their official recovery process.
- [ ] Preserve messages, receipts, dates, and transaction references.
- [ ] If identity information was exposed, use the official recovery process and consider credit freezes. [Identity theft](guide/18-accounts-identity-and-privacy.md#183-start-an-identity-theft-recovery-file-immediately) · [Credit freezes](guide/18-accounts-identity-and-privacy.md#182-freeze-credit-when-you-are-not-applying-for-it).
- [ ] Report through the appropriate official channel; do not pay a stranger who promises guaranteed recovery.
- [ ] Tell a trusted person if that helps you act. Shame can delay useful steps.

## 8. Disability is changing my work or daily life

- [ ] Record functional limitations and the support needed, alongside medical information.
- [ ] Ask about concrete workplace or service accommodations. [Work](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#134-request-accommodations-and-track-discrimination-deadlines) · [Access](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#223-request-the-access-you-need-in-concrete-terms).
- [ ] Check SSDI, SSI, and applicable state programs separately. [Income support](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#221-ask-about-both-ssdi-and-ssi-when-disability-limits-work).
- [ ] Ask about home and community-based care, waitlists, and interim options. [Care at home](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#222-ask-your-state-about-care-at-home-before-assuming-an-institution-is-required).
- [ ] Before changing earnings or transferring savings, obtain benefits guidance. [Working with benefits](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#225-explore-work-support-without-guessing-what-earnings-will-do-to-benefits) · [ABLE accounts](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#224-check-able-eligibility-before-building-savings-in-the-wrong-account).
- [ ] Keep every notice and calendar each appeal or reporting deadline.

## 9. My parent needs more care

- [ ] Ask what help is needed with everyday activities and what the person wants.
- [ ] Review health coverage and plan separately for custodial care. [Long-term care](guide/21-aging-care-and-end-of-life.md#213-budget-for-everyday-care-separately-from-medicare).
- [ ] Identify the people legally authorized to help with medical and financial decisions. [Decision makers](guide/21-aging-care-and-end-of-life.md#214-choose-decision-makers-while-you-can-express-your-wishes).
- [ ] Review medicines and fall risks with appropriate help. [Medication list](guide/05-chronic-illness.md#051-keep-one-medication-list-and-check-changes) · [Fall prevention](guide/05-chronic-illness.md#055-reduce-fall-risks-before-an-injury-limits-independence).
- [ ] Ask about public home-care support and caregiver relief where available. [Home services](guide/22-disability-and-independent-living.md#222-ask-your-state-about-care-at-home-before-assuming-an-institution-is-required).
- [ ] Assign realistic responsibilities; check leave options before a caregiver leaves work. [Protected leave](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#132-ask-about-protected-leave-before-using-up-your-options).

## 10. I am starting adult life

- [ ] Locate identity documents and store copies securely. [Document folder](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md#362-build-a-secure-identity-document-folder-before-an-appointment).
- [ ] Confirm health coverage through school or work transitions. [Coverage](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md#361-confirm-health-coverage-through-each-life-transition).
- [ ] Use verified first-job onboarding channels. [Paperwork](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md#365-complete-first-job-paperwork-only-through-verified-onboarding).
- [ ] Match a bank account to your cash flow and fees. [Banking](guide/07-cash-flow-banking-credit.md#073-make-your-checking-account-fit-your-habits).
- [ ] Compare school, transfer routes, and paid apprenticeships using real costs and outcomes. [Education and training](guide/15-education-and-student-loans.md).
- [ ] Check your official election office's registration rules if eligible, and name a medical decision-maker under your state's process. [Voting](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md#363-check-voter-registration-through-your-official-election-office) · [Medical authority](guide/36-adult-life-and-civic-paperwork.md#364-name-a-medical-decision-maker-before-an-emergency).

## 11. Someone in my family died

- [ ] If the death was unexpected or the situation is an emergency, contact emergency services. For an expected death under hospice care, follow the care team's instructions.
- [ ] Ask the funeral provider about death certificates and which notifications it handles. Request an itemized price list. [Funeral costs](guide/21-aging-care-and-end-of-life.md#215-ask-for-itemized-funeral-prices-before-choosing-a-package).
- [ ] Locate the will and identify the person with lawful authority to administer the estate. Do not distribute property or use the person's accounts just because you know a password.
- [ ] Keep bills and financial records together. Get advice before agreeing to pay an estate debt personally; co-signing, joint debt, and state spousal rules can change responsibility.
- [ ] Use the [official notification checklist](https://www.usa.gov/report-a-death) and [FTC guidance on a deceased relative's debt](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/debts-and-deceased-relatives). State probate procedures determine the next legal steps.
- [ ] Divide the paperwork into urgent deadlines and decisions that can wait. [Grief and major setbacks](guide/27-grief-burnout-setbacks.md).

## 12. A storm, heat wave, or wildfire is approaching

- [ ] Follow current local emergency instructions, including evacuation orders.
- [ ] Arrange transport, a destination, medication access, and a contact plan that includes pets and access needs.
- [ ] Prepare drinking water and essential supplies. [Household preparation](guide/24-travel-and-emergency-preparation.md#241-start-emergency-supplies-with-drinking-water-and-a-household-plan).
- [ ] Keep generators outdoors and away from openings; check carbon monoxide alarms. [Carbon monoxide safety](guide/24-travel-and-emergency-preparation.md#242-install-working-carbon-monoxide-alarms-and-keep-generators-outside).
- [ ] Prepare cooling or cleaner indoor air as appropriate. [Heat](guide/38-food-home-and-climate-safety.md#384-arrange-cooling-before-a-heat-wave) · [Wildfire smoke](guide/38-food-home-and-climate-safety.md#385-prepare-a-cleaner-indoor-room-for-wildfire-smoke).
- [ ] Keep policy and claim contact information accessible; document damage only when safe. [Coverage gaps](guide/29-insurance-large-losses.md#294-identify-flood-and-earthquake-gaps-before-a-disaster).
