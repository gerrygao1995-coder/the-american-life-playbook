## Changes in this edition

The prose was newly written in English. Mainland-China-specific legal, tax, health-insurance, social-insurance, and administrative pathways were replaced with U.S. federal and local verification routes. The organization was expanded to 40 topics, including U.S. veteran benefits, disaster coverage, retirement drawdown, and household environmental hazards. We added 12 scenario checklists, 10 communication scripts, a 30-day starting plan, a source ledger, and a new offline reader. Rule/Guidance/Practice labels replace a single combined evidence scale. No upstream software or graphic assets were copied.

The upstream README listed **672 recommendations in 34 themes** when consulted; this edition has **200 focused plays in 40 topics**. This is a selective adaptation and reorganization, not a complete translation or a claim to contain more recommendations. The map below shows thematic correspondence, not one-to-one replacement or equivalent depth. Some source topics have only brief coverage here, and some specific recommendations are omitted.
