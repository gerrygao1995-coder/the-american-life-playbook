# Ten scripts for the first conversation

Replace brackets with your facts. These are conversation starters, not legal filings, medical orders, or a substitute for a required form. Verify the recipient before sending. Keep account numbers, medical details, identity documents, and other private information out of public issues. A message does not itself pause collection, preserve legal rights, or extend a deadline.

## 1. Medical bill review

> I am reviewing the bill for [date of service]. Please send an itemized statement and explain [specific charge or mismatch]. What financial assistance is available, how do I apply, and what is the deadline? Please tell me whether collection can be paused while the review or application is pending and confirm any arrangement in writing.

Record the billing reference, requested documents, and response date. [Read the conditions](guide/04-health-insurance.md#044-request-bill-review-and-financial-assistance-before-borrowing).

## 2. Insurance denial clarification

> I received a denial dated [date], reference [number]. What exact reason and plan provision were used? Please send the appeal instructions, submission address, deadline, and documents needed. Is an expedited process available for my circumstances, and what must my clinician submit? Please confirm receipt of any appeal I send.

Use the formal appeal procedure; this inquiry alone may not count as an appeal. [Read the conditions](guide/04-health-insurance.md#045-appeal-a-denial-using-the-reason-and-deadline).

## 3. Planned-care estimate

> I am considering [service] on [date]. Please identify the expected services and billing providers and give me an estimate. Which charges are separate? I will also confirm coverage and network status with my insurer. What could change the amount, and whom should I contact if the final bill differs?

An estimate and a network-directory listing are not guarantees of payment. [Read the conditions](guide/04-health-insurance.md#043-confirm-the-network-and-expected-charges-before-planned-care).

## 4. Creditor hardship request

> My circumstances changed because [brief explanation]. I can currently afford [realistic amount] after essential expenses. What hardship options are available? Please explain interest, fees, credit reporting, term length, and what happens at the end. I would like written terms before I agree.

Avoid promising payments you cannot make or authorizing withdrawals without understanding them. [Prioritize essentials](guide/08-debt-scams-setbacks.md#081-protect-essentials-when-there-is-not-enough-money) · [Compare qualified help](guide/08-debt-scams-setbacks.md#084-compare-qualified-help-before-buying-debt-relief).

## 5. Collection verification inquiry

> I received your notice dated [date]. Please identify the original creditor, itemized amount, current owner, and the process and deadline for disputing this debt or requesting verification. Please send the information through [appropriate verified channel]. I am reviewing the claim and have not agreed to a payment arrangement.

If you dispute the debt, follow the notice's legally applicable dispute procedure and timing. This generic inquiry is not a court answer. [Verify a collection demand](guide/08-debt-scams-setbacks.md#083-verify-collection-demands-before-paying) · [Recognize a summons](guide/17-legal-and-consumer-problems.md#172-distinguish-a-collection-notice-from-a-court-summons).

## 6. Rental repair request

> I am reporting [problem] at [unit] first noticed on [date]. It affects [specific use or safety issue]. Attached are [photos or prior messages]. Please confirm receipt, the next steps, and the expected repair timing. I can discuss access at [options]. Please keep me updated in writing.

Follow the lease and local notice procedure. Ask local legal aid before withholding rent or arranging deductions. [Keep rental records](guide/11-renting-buying-home.md#112-read-the-lease-and-document-the-condition) · [Find legal help](guide/17-legal-and-consumer-problems.md#171-find-the-right-legal-help-and-identify-the-next-deadline).

## 7. Workplace accommodation request

> I would like to discuss an accommodation for a limitation affecting [work task]. A change such as [specific adjustment] may help me perform the job. Please explain the process, the appropriate confidential contact, and any necessary documentation. I am open to discussing effective alternatives.

Give information through the appropriate confidential channel. Eligibility, documentation, and the employer's obligations depend on the applicable law. [Read the conditions](guide/13-work-pay-and-benefits.md#134-request-accommodations-and-track-discrimination-deadlines).

## 8. Job-offer negotiation

> Thank you for the offer for [role]. Based on [relevant experience, responsibilities, and market information], I would like to discuss [specific base pay or other term]. [Concrete contribution] is particularly relevant to the role. Could we review this before [decision date]? Please also send the benefits costs and final terms in writing.

Use truthful evidence and a clear request; leave room for the employer to respond. [Negotiation play](guide/33-career-growth-and-negotiation.md#332-make-a-specific-compensation-request-before-accepting).

## 9. Contractor scope clarification

> Before I sign, please specify materials, tasks, exclusions, permit responsibility, payment milestones, expected timing, cleanup, and warranty terms. How will changes be approved and priced? Please provide the license and insurance details I can verify and identify who will perform the work.

Verify through relevant authorities and insurers; a logo or screenshot is not enough. [Contractor checks](guide/31-repairs-utilities-contracts.md#311-verify-the-contractor-and-permit-requirements-yourself) · [Written scope](guide/31-repairs-utilities-contracts.md#312-put-the-scope-and-changes-in-writing).

## 10. Consumer complaint escalation

> On [date], I purchased [product or service] for [amount]. The problem is [specific discrepancy]. I contacted [department] on [dates], reference [numbers]. I am requesting [specific reasonable remedy]. Attached are the relevant records. Please respond by [reasonable requested date], or tell me which department can resolve this.

A requested date does not change a legal deadline. Choose the correct business, regulator, card-dispute, or court process for the issue. [Escalation](guide/35-travel-and-consumer-disputes.md#355-escalate-with-a-clear-record-and-a-specific-requested-remedy) · [Billing disputes](guide/35-travel-and-consumer-disputes.md#353-preserve-the-credit-card-billing-dispute-deadline).
