# 22 Disability and independent living

Pursue income, care, access, and work support separately. Qualification for one program does not settle another.

## 22.1 Ask about both SSDI and SSI when disability limits work

SSDI is tied to insured status based on covered earnings; SSI considers limited income and resources as well as age, blindness, or disability. Some people qualify for both. Start with Social Security, keep records of functional limitations and treatment, and save every decision notice. Follow the appeal instructions and deadline on a denial. A diagnosis alone does not establish eligibility for every program.

- **Cost and effort:** Applying through SSA is free; gathering records and review take time.
- **Payoff:** Identifies possible income support and preserves the chance to challenge an error.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [SSA comparison of disability programs](https://www.ssa.gov/redbook/eng/overview-disability.htm)

## 22.2 Ask your state about care at home before assuming an institution is required

Contact Medicaid or the state disability services agency about home and community-based services. Ask which program fits the person’s age and needs, what financial and functional tests apply, whether enrollment is capped, and what alternatives exist while waiting. Some waivers can cover personal care, respite, or other support. Benefits and openings differ; getting Medicaid does not automatically guarantee every home-care service.

- **Cost and effort:** Application and assessment effort; cost sharing and availability vary.
- **Payoff:** May make support at home possible and reduce unpaid caregiver strain.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule. [Medicaid home and community-based waivers](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/home-community-based-services/home-community-based-services-authorities/home-community-based-services-1915c)

## 22.3 Request the access you need in concrete terms

Describe the barrier and the change that would help: accessible documents, communication support, a usable entrance, or an adjustment to a service. Keep a dated record of requests and responses. Disability rights apply in many settings, but the applicable law differs: housing, air travel, employment, and public services do not all use the same complaint process. Use the relevant agency instead of buying an unofficial disability certificate.

- **Cost and effort:** Requests are usually free; resolution may require follow-up.
- **Payoff:** Provides a clearer route to participation and a record if you must escalate.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [Department of Justice overview of disability rights](https://www.ada.gov/topics/intro-to-ada/)

## 22.4 Check ABLE eligibility before building savings in the wrong account

An ABLE account can hold money for qualified disability expenses with special tax and benefit treatment. Starting in 2026, the qualifying disability must generally have begun before age 46, alongside the other eligibility requirements. Compare program fees, contribution rules, SSI balance treatment, and possible Medicaid recovery. Keep expense records and get benefits guidance before a large transfer; ABLE protection has conditions and limits.

- **Cost and effort:** Program fees vary; allow time to review benefit effects.
- **Payoff:** May allow useful savings without the same treatment as ordinary countable resources.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule. [SSA ABLE accounts — 2026 edition](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi/spotlights/spot-able.html)

## 22.5 Explore work support without guessing what earnings will do to benefits

Contact your state vocational rehabilitation agency or a local Center for Independent Living about training and support. If you receive Social Security disability benefits, ask about Ticket to Work and individualized benefits counseling before changing work hours. Report earnings as required and save confirmation. Support programs can help, but each person’s cash benefits and health coverage may respond differently to earnings.

- **Cost and effort:** Public services may be free; eligibility and appointment availability vary.
- **Payoff:** Helps plan a realistic return to work with fewer benefit surprises.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Guidance + Rule. [USAGov disability employment and training routes](https://www.usa.gov/disability-jobs-training)
