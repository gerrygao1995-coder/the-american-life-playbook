# 37 Military and veteran benefits

Military service creates distinct benefits and obligations. Verify the program that applies to your service history and household.

## 37.1 Use an accredited representative for a VA claim

Find an accredited Veterans Service Organization representative through VA and ask whether they can help with your claim or decision review. Accredited VSO claim assistance is free; attorneys and claims agents may charge under applicable rules. Keep your own copies of service records, medical evidence, submissions, and decision letters. Be cautious about anyone guaranteeing a rating or demanding broad access to your accounts.

- **Cost and effort:** VSO assistance is free; evidence gathering takes time.
- **Payoff:** Provides a legitimate route through a complicated claims process.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Guidance. [VA accredited representatives and free VSO help](https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/)

## 37.2 Check VA health care eligibility directly

Review VA’s eligibility criteria and apply if your service history may qualify. Ask how enrollment, priority group, copayments, and other insurance affect your care. Do not assume that having a private plan disqualifies you, or that every veteran automatically receives every service free. Keep your care team informed about medications and treatment from other systems so records and prescriptions do not become fragmented.

- **Cost and effort:** Eligibility inquiry is free; costs depend on enrollment and services.
- **Payoff:** May open access to care you have not been using.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [VA health care eligibility](https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/)

## 37.3 Check what your GI Bill actually pays before choosing a school

Use VA’s comparison tools and your Statement of Benefits to check the approved program, remaining entitlement, tuition coverage, and living-cost support. Ask the school’s certifying official for a written estimate and compare graduation, transfer, and job outcomes. Approval for benefits does not guarantee that a school is a good investment or that every fee will be covered. Review consequences before changing programs or withdrawing.

- **Cost and effort:** Free comparison; schooling and uncovered living costs may be substantial.
- **Payoff:** Protects a limited education benefit from a poor program choice.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [VA GI Bill benefits and comparison resources](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/)

## 37.4 Compare a VA-backed mortgage with other offers

Check your Certificate of Eligibility and compare lender offers using the same home price, loan amount, and closing date. Ask about the funding fee and any exemption, interest rate, closing costs, and monthly taxes and insurance. A possible low or zero down payment does not remove the cost of owning a home. Keep repair reserves and compare with renting if your next move may be soon.

- **Cost and effort:** Eligibility tools are free; borrowing and closing costs vary by loan.
- **Payoff:** Uses the benefit as one option in a full affordability comparison.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [VA-backed home loans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/)

## 37.5 Ask legal assistance about service-related debt and lease protections

When entering active duty or receiving qualifying orders, ask military legal assistance whether the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act applies to existing debts, housing, vehicle leases, or court proceedings. Keep orders and written notices. Eligibility, notice requirements, and timing matter; a protection for one contract does not cancel all obligations. Contact the lender or landlord through a documented process instead of simply stopping payment.

- **Cost and effort:** Military legal assistance may be free for eligible people.
- **Payoff:** Can preserve protections that are easy to miss during a move or deployment.
- **Priority:** When relevant.
- **Basis:** Rule + Practice. [DOJ servicemember financial and housing rights](https://www.justice.gov/servicemembers/financial-and-housing-rights-0)
